# visualbrief 0.1.0

* First release.
* `vb_campaign()` and `vb_read_csv()` standardise exports from any platform.
* `vb_summarise()`, `vb_trend()` and `vb_compare()`: totals, rates with Wilson
  intervals, costs, trends by day, week or month, and comparisons with
  Newcombe intervals and Holm-adjusted tests.
* `vb_brand()`, `vb_brand_yml()` and `vb_check_contrast()`: an accessible
  visual identity, readable from `_brand.yml`.
* `vb_plot_rates()`, `vb_plot_trend()` and `vb_report()`: branded charts and a
  self-contained HTML report; `vb_quarto_template()` for an editable version.
* `campaign_demo`: a synthetic campaign with documented effects.
